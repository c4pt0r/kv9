# Vector-receipt point-read/mixed screen evidence

This compact bundle preserves the accepted 24-cohort screen of candidate `7c8cd25e9531809c9d5bd40faee116a7d6d5a3ff` against the selected CRC server `ca0002c7f8e9ee6f595efcc9f4151085ccce87cb` and fixed v3 native/Redis clients `0be806d9671e2c50701a64aa7889c8859b7648ba`.

The protocol covers point GET/PUT at 50% and 100% reads, concurrency 1 and 64, and two complete forward/reverse repetitions of ten seconds per cohort. The separate twelve-cohort smoke used two seconds. [READOUT.md](READOUT.md) and [PER-REPEAT.md](PER-REPEAT.md) are verbatim copies of the original statistics outputs; every raw histogram and both repetitions are retained.

At c64, mixed throughput rose 2.589% with improved mean and p99 in both repetitions; pure-GET throughput fell 0.349%. Neither c1 workload improved throughput or mean, but c1 pure-GET p99 improved in both repetitions (pooled interval 50.688–51.199 to 49.664–50.175 microseconds). The measurement is accepted; the candidate remains experimental without general promotion. Two repetitions do not establish statistical significance or sustained capacity, and this screen does not cover the full 72-cohort workload protocol. KV9 quorum/sync calls used tmpfs WAL; standalone in-memory Redis has different durability semantics.

The archive contains all 24 original report/cleanup/requested-config/resource-coverage sets; the unchanged accepted audit and full original input inventory; frozen screen preparation with pending drafts, diffs and contract transcripts; original build manifests, Cargo and cache receipts; smoke/timing plans and matrices; isolation and root stage receipts; and frozen statistics code, inputs and complete results. The original audit accepted 49,724,855 measurement calls and attempts, all successful, 80 exited lifetimes, 48 qualifying drains, 48 voter/listener bindings and 4,677 resource samples. Setup attempts remain separately accounted in the original reports.
